# Changelog

Releases of the modules in `github.com/nfltr/relay`: `proto/vX.Y.Z`,
`client/vX.Y.Z`, `org/vX.Y.Z`. The API changes each makes on purpose since
its last tag are `api/<module>-<tag>.txt` (`make check-api`).

## Unreleased

- org: a join takes no root older than the one its token was made under (lane SECFIX, review S9; `spec/enrol/Join.tla` `JoinRootNotOlder`, `JoinOlder.cfg`, mutant `older-root`). Tokens are `nfltr-org-token-v3.<org>.<id>.<secret>.<root NotBefore>` (`orgpki.TokenRootNotBefore`); `orgenrol.Join` refuses a root, the pinned one too, whose NotBefore is before the token's, so the relay cannot serve a superseded root (whose keys may be anyone's) to a joining machine. Earlier (v2) tokens are refused by name: ask for a new token. Residuals: a root superseded after the token was made (make new tokens after a rotation), and a sign-in join (`JoinSignIn`), which names no root.
- client, org: a service may bound its sessions by the age of the CRL its machine holds (lane SECFIX item 8, review section 4 A; `spec/enrol/CrlCarriers.tla` FreshBound, FreshQuiet, `CrlCarriersFresh.cfg`, mutants `fresh-ignored`, `fresh-short`): `orgmachine.(*Machine).Fresh(d)` is the machine for one service whose `CheckClient` refuses a client once the CRL of its issuer was signed `d` ago and sets the admission's until to that ThisUpdate + `d` (`orgid.(*Verifier).FreshBound`), so a held session is cut at the bound while the relay withholds every newer CRL, and a newer CRL taken moves it. `d` is `orgid.FreshMin` (2 h, `CRLWarnAge`) to `orgpki.CRLLife` (24 h): below 2 h the hourly re-sign and the copy's lag cut sessions while the issuer is up. nfltr's `nfltr up` key `fresh:` sets it; without it nothing changes. Additions only: no migration (`api/client-v0.27.1.txt`, `api/org-v0.4.1.txt`).
- client, org: a machine's newest CRL number, rules number and status time, and the newest list's ThisUpdate, hold across a restart (lane SECFIX, review S10, S18): `orgid.Identity` and `orgmachine.Machine` keep them in `marks.json` beside `crl.der` (`orgid.MarksFile`, `LoadMarks`, `KeepMarks`) and take them as floors at load, under the root they were taken under. A fresh process refuses the relay's older list or status, also when `crl.der` lapsed or (a client) no status was kept; a `crl.der` put back to an older one is not taken. A clock more than 5 minutes before the newest list's ThisUpdate fails every check closed ("the clock ... is before the newest revocation list"), so a clock set back does not honour a lapsed list again. They guard against the relay, not against a process of the machine's user.
- org: a people act is taken once (lane SECFIX, review S7; `spec/proverif/people.pv` query 14, mutant `act-no-head`): `orgpki.PeopleAct.Head` names the issuer log's head the signer read from the status, and `Issuer.ApplyPeople` refuses an act whose head is before the roster's last change (an invite, a role, a removal) or is no head of the log. Before, the relay could send an applied act again within its `OrderLife` (a demoted admin promoted again by the replay of the promotion). A signer of people acts reads `Status.Head` first; an act racing another one is refused and signed again.
- client, org: the access rules end with the root that signed them (lane SECFIX, review S5; relay-server `spec/GroupMirror.tla` `RulesFollowRotation`, `GroupMirrorRotate.cfg`): `orgid.Rules` holds the root a status was taken under; after a rotation no rule of the old root admits, and the new root's status is taken whatever number the old root's holder put on its rules (before, they survived the rotation, and a planted number kept every new-root policy out until restart). `orgmachine.Machine` removes `status.json` at a rotation. A status is taken only from an issuer certificate the verifier would admit (unexpired, not on the root's CRL of issuers), not one with a bare signature check. API: `orgid.Rules.Take(msg, *Verifier, now)` (was root and organisation id); `Rules.Admit`, unused, is deleted (`orgid.AdmitClient` is the check).
- client: a root-revoked issuer is refused at every chain position, and an issuer's certificate is never a leaf (lane SECFIX, review S4; `spec/proverif/README.md` `hosted.rootcrl`): `orgid.Verifier` checked the root's CRL of issuers only on chains of three or more, so `TakeCRL` took a CRL the revoked issuer signed and `Check` admitted the issuer's own certificate presented as a client's (its own issuer, inside its own groups, under its own CRL). `Check` and the grant check now take only a non-CA leaf under an issuer under the root.
- client: identity to the app is set on every request of a session until the app switches protocol (lane SECFIX, review S2, S17; `spec/proverif/svc_access.pv` README "What the code must do after the checked function returns", `BIND:svc.hdrwho`): `agent.IdentityHTTP` and the E2EE stream path go on as bytes only after the app's 101 to an upgrade or 2xx to a CONNECT (`switchWatch` reads a copy of the responses), not on the client's `Upgrade` header alone: before, an app that ignored the upgrade and answered 200 got the client's later requests unrewritten, with its own `Nfltr-Person`, `Nfltr-Machine`, `Nfltr-Groups` (and on the stream path its own `Host` and forwarded headers). The client's `Nfltr-*` trailers and `Connection` tokens naming an identity header are dropped (a `Connection: Nfltr-Person` deleted the agent's own header). After a switch under `IdentityHTTP` each read goes on as it comes (a WebSocket frame waited in a 4 KiB buffer).
- client, org: per-session audit (lane SA5, nfltr `docs/decisions/service-access-2026-11-25.md` "SA5"; `spec/proverif/svc_access.pv` query 7, `BIND:svc.auditsig`): a serving `orgmachine.Machine` signs a numbered, hash-chained line at each organisation session's open and close (`client/orgaudit`: `Record`, `Line`, the signed `Head`, the one check `Checker`; metadata only: who, the service, the rule or grant that admitted it (`orgid.AdmittedBy`), when, how long, the plaintext bytes (`(*tlsend.Conn).Bytes`), why it ended, the path) to `orgmachine.AuditFile`. The `orgenrol` keeper ships them HPKE-sealed to the issuer (`orgpki.Request.Audit`, `SealAudit`, `OpenAudit`) on the enrol drop box: the relay-server mailbox is reused unchanged and never reads a record. The issuer appends what verifies to `orgenrol.SessionsFile`, logs a refusal (`orgpki.ActAuditRefused`), reports gaps, alterations, withheld lines and silent machines (`orgenrol.ReadAudit`) and exports the lines to `orgenrol.SetAuditSink` (a file, or an https URL). An agent's `Org` that implements `agent.OrgAuditor` is told each admitted session. Additions only: no migration (`api/client-v0.27.0.txt`, `api/org-v0.4.0.txt`).
- proto, client: the agent wire's proto package is `nfltr.relay.v1` (gRPC paths `/nfltr.relay.v1.Dispatcher/...`; the Go package is still `pb`) and the client generation is 4. A hard cut, no older name is served: a relay refuses an older client's call with "this nfltr is too old for this relay: run `nfltr update`" (`agentwire.ClientGenerationTooOld`), and this client names a relay that does not serve the package as too old (`agentwire.RelayTooOld`, `grpcclient.ClientIdentity`), which `rpcclient.FatalClass` stops on. Migration: update every client and relay together.
- client: identity to the app (lane SA2, nfltr `docs/decisions/service-access-2026-11-25.md` section 3, `spec/proverif/svc_access.pv` queries 5-6, rows `BIND:svc.hdrwho`, `BIND:svc.aud`): `agent.Service.Identity` on an organisation-mode tcp service tells its target who called, from the admitted client certificate only. `agent.IdentityHTTP`: every HTTP/1 request reaches the target without the client's `Nfltr-*` and `X-Nfltr-*` headers (underscores read as dashes) and with `Nfltr-Machine`, `Nfltr-Person` (a person's device), `Nfltr-Groups`, `Nfltr-Service` and `Nfltr-Assertion`, a JWT (EdDSA, RFC 8037) under the machine's organisation key: `iss` the machine, `aud` the service, `sub` the person or the caller's machine, `machine`, `groups`, `exp` one minute; `agent.JWKSHandler` serves at `agent.JWKSPath` (`/.well-known/nfltr/jwks.json`) the key set of the current machine key an app verifies it with (a renewal is a new key and kid; the next request signs with it). `agent.IdentityProxyV2`: a PROXY protocol v2 header (LOCAL, no addresses) with TLVs `agent.TLVMachine`, `TLVPerson`, `TLVGroups`, `TLVService` before the session's first byte. Off by default.
- client, org: an organisation's access rules are the root's signed policy (lane SA3, nfltr `docs/decisions/service-access-2026-11-25.md` "2. Policy"; relay-server `spec/GroupMirror.tla` PeerRuleSigned, PeerRuleStaleBound; `spec/proverif/svc_access.pv` query 4, `BIND:svc.rulesig`, `BIND:svc.apprpol`). The root signs an `orgid.Policy` (rules: a `group:`, `machine:` or `person:` principal may use `<service>` or `*` at a `machine:` or `group:` place; approvers per service) with `orgpki.SignPolicy` on its machine; the issuer takes it (`Issuer.TakePolicy`, carried as `Request.Policy`, `orgenrol.SendPolicy`; a newer `Number` only, logged as `ActPolicy`) and carries it in every signed status (`Status.Policy`). A serving agent reads the relay's status copy (`orgid.Follow`, through `orgid.StatusCopy`), takes it with `(*orgid.Identity).TakeStatus` or `(*orgmachine.Machine).TakeStatus` (both `orgid.Rules`: signed by the root's issuer, a higher rules `Number`, or the same under a later `At`; the machine keeps it in `status.json`, `orgid.StatusFile`, and its `orgenrol` keeper reads the copy too; `orgmachinetest.Org.Allow` for tests) and admits a client by a rule while that status's CRL `NextUpdate` is ahead: fail closed without one. Breaking, no migration (no users; hard cut): `agent.Service.AllowGroups`, `agent.Config.TCPE2EEAllowGroups` and `nfnet.ListenOptions.AllowGroups` are removed, and `agent.Org.CheckClient` takes the session's services, not groups (`api/client-v0.25.4.txt`, `api/org-v0.3.0.txt`).
- org, client: grants, time-limited access to one service approved by a person (lane SA4, nfltr `docs/decisions/service-access-2026-11-25.md`; `spec/grant/Grant.tla`, `spec/proverif/svc_access.pv` rows `BIND:svc.grantsig`, `svc.grantwho`, `svc.grantsvc`, `svc.apprall`): the issuer signs a grant certificate for the requester's own key once an approver the root's policy names approves the request's digest (`orgpki.GrantAsk`, `GrantApprove`, `GrantDeny`, `GrantRevoke`, `(*Issuer).ApplyGrant`, `orgenrol.SendGrant`; deny is final; a revoke, or removing the grant's person or machine, lists its serial in the next CRL; `orgpki.MaxGrantFor`); a session presents it after its chain (`(*orgid.Identity).ClientCertificate`) and the serving agent admits by it for that service and place until it ends (`orgid.AdmitClient`, `(*orgid.Verifier).CheckGrant`). Additions only (`api/client-v0.25.4.txt`, `api/org-v0.3.0.txt`).
- org: an SSH host certificate keeps a life of its own, `orgpki.HostLife` 24 h, step A of lane ORGCERT72b (`spec/enrol/CertRide.tla` HostRevokeBound, `CertRideDay.cfg`): OpenSSH reads no CRL, so its life is ssh's bound on a revoked machine while a relay withholds every CRL read. `orgpki.Issuer.HostCert` signs it until `HostLife` after the issue or the machine certificate's NotAfter if sooner; `orgpki.CheckHostCert` accepts a host certificate no longer than that and not past the machine certificate (it required the machine certificate's exact lifetime); `orgmachine.(*Machine).RenewDue` is the earlier of the two half lives. `orgpki.MachineLife` stays 24 h, so every certificate a step-A issuer signs still has equal lifetimes and clients before it keep renewing (`TestStepAHostCertPassesTheOldCheck`). Release gate: step B (`MachineLife` 72 h, lane ORGCERT72) lands one release after step A has reached every machine, the canary and our own (every organisation is ours today); a client before step A refuses a step-B renewal. No migration (`api/org-v0.3.0.txt`).
- org, client: an organisation's short name, signed by its root (lane SA1, nfltr `docs/decisions/service-access-2026-11-25.md` D2): `orgpki.NewRoot` and `orgpki.CrossSign` take a trailing `...orgpki.RootOption`; `orgpki.WithShortName(short)` signs it as the root's URI SAN `nfltr://<org id>.internal/name/<short>`, the Subject unchanged, so a rotation carries it over and an organisation made without one gets one by a re-sign under the same root key (key, Subject, id and every chain unchanged). `orgid.ValidShortName` (one lowercase DNS label starting with a letter, not `o-`), `orgid.ShortName` (the root's name SAN, else its id) and `(*orgid.Identity).ShortName` are additions (`api/client-v0.25.3.txt`). For org the variadic parameter changes the two functions' types (`api/org-v0.2.9.txt`); no migration: every call compiles unchanged, only a function value of the old type does not.
- org, client: an issuer outage no longer stops the fleet (lane ISSUEROUT, nfltr `docs/decisions/issuer-outage-2026-11-25.md`, `spec/enrol/CrlCarriers.tla` PushBound, RideOutage, QuietWhileIssuerUp, WarnWhileIssuerDown): `orgpki.CRLLife` is 24 h (was 1 h) and the issuer re-signs every `orgpki.CRLEvery` (1 h; was at a third of the life left), so members keep a current CRL through an issuer outage of up to about 22 h 45 min while a revoke still reaches them at its push; `orgenrol.PublishedLate` judges a published CRL overdue at `CRLEvery` + `PublishGrace`. Members warn: `orgid.CRLWarnAge` (2 h), `orgid.(*Verifier).CRLStale` and `orgmachine.(*Machine).CRLStale` (the warning naming the fix), logged every follow round by `orgid.Follow` and the organisation Keeper (`orgid.WarnStale`). `orgpki.Issuer.Restore` takes a published CRL signed `orgpki.RestoreQuiet` (2 h) ago instead of a lapsed one (a day now); `orgpki.ErrNotLapsed` is `orgpki.ErrNotQuiet` (migration: rename; its only callers were tests).
- client: `machinecred`'s HTTP connections close before the certificate their handshake presented expires (5 min before its NotAfter, at it for one opened later), so a long-lived process's next call presents the newest, an expired one renewed first (lane ISSUERKEEP, relay-server `spec/MachineAuth.tla` KeptConnValid). Before, a kept HTTP/2 connection presented the expired certificate and every call was refused (403) until a restart: the organisation issuer's outage of 2026-11-22. Adds `(*Credential).TransportFor` (a base TLS configuration, as presence's `--cert-sha256`); no migration (`api/client-v0.25.3.txt`).
- org: the people layer in the issuer (lane PEOPLE2, `spec/people/People.tla`, `spec/proverif/people.pv`; nfltr `docs/decisions/people-layer-2026-11-25.md`): a roster with roles owner, admin and member in the issuer's log (`invite`, the join's `issued` line naming its invite and role, `role`, a person's `revoked` line with the actor and each machine `Filed` for its relay revoke and address rotation). Acts are `PeopleRequest`s (`SignPeople`, `Issuer.ApplyPeople`, carried as `Request.People`; the answer's `Removal`): the owner's signed by the root, an admin's by the key of one of the admin's machines, the role read from the log at each request. An invite is an Ed25519 seed the inviter makes (`NewInvite`; the log holds its public key; 7 days, `InviteLife`); a sign-in enrols a new key only with one, proved over the request's keys (`SignInRequest.Invite`, `ProveInvite`; `orgenrol.JoinSignIn` takes the invite). Breaking, no migration (no organisation used sign-in rules): APPID2's domain rules are gone (`SignInRule.EmailDomains`, `ValidDomain`; owner decision 2026-11-25: invites only), a person's second device by sign-in alone is refused (a code from `MakeDeviceToken`), and `Issuer.RevokePerson` returns the `Removal` (`api/org-v0.2.9.txt`). SCIM is later (a deprovision becomes a removal an admin confirms).

- client: `nfnet.ListenOptions.RelayCap` sets an `Address`'s daily cap on the bytes its relay carries for sessions not opened with this account, as the agent's label `nfltr.nf1.relay_cap.<service>` that relay-core reads (`core/nf1cap`, lane ANONCAP, nfltr `docs/decisions/nf1-relay-cap-2026-11-25.md`); nil keeps the relay's default (`nf1.relay_cap`). An addition only: no migration (`api/client-v0.25.3.txt`).
- org: a renewal asked again is for the same key and is answered again (lane CANARYCERT, `spec/enrol/Enrol.tla` Request, Repeat): the keeper keeps the renewal's key in `orgenrol.RenewKeyFile` (`renew.key` in the machine directory) until its answer is installed, and the issuer renews an expired certificate in the grace whose only successor renewed it for the request's key. Before, an answer the machine did not take superseded its certificate, refused for good once expired. Adds `orgenrol.RenewKeyFile` and `orgmachine.(*Machine).Dir`; no migration (`api/org-v0.2.8.txt`). Machines already superseded (no key kept for the newest serial) join again once.
- client: `agent.TransportStats` gains `WindowGrowths` and `WindowGrown` from the `link.ConnStats` it embeds (link's `FeatureGrow`, link v0.14.0): `WindowGrowths` counts the sessions' windows grown; `WindowGrown` is a gauge, the growth past `Config.Window` the open sessions hold, summed (at most `Config.WindowBudget`). Additions only: no migration (`api/client-v0.25.1.txt`).
